Problem Statement
Glaucoma, the "silent thief of sight," affects over 80 million people worldwide and often goes undetected until irreversible vision loss occurs. Current detection methods are expensive, invasive, and inaccessible in rural areas. The severity lies in its permanent blindness potential if untreated. Lack of early, affordable screening tools leads to delayed diagnosis, resulting in avoidable blindness and reduced quality of life.

Existing Solution
Traditional methods include Goldmann Applanation Tonometry and Ophthalmoscopic Examinations, which require trained specialists, costly equipment, and hospital visits. Gaps:
Expensive, non-portable devices.
Invasive and uncomfortable tests.
Limited availability in rural/remote regions.
Dependence on skilled ophthalmologists.
No real-time integration with digital platforms for awareness or monitoring.


Proposed Solution
Our venture Airton introduces a portable, affordable, and non-invasive air-puff tonometer integrated with AI-powered glaucoma detection. The device uses controlled air puffs to measure intraocular pressure (IOP), while the mobile app & cloud integration provide instant analysis, patient history tracking, and referrals. The technology stack includes Arduino/ESP32 microcontrollers, pressure sensors, AI models, and a mobile/web app, making early glaucoma detection accessible to all.
