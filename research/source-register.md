# Source register

Reviewed 4 October 2026. IDs below are used throughout the dossier. Sources are
primary clinical guidance, original papers, regulator publications, official
standards catalogues, manufacturer documents, or official technical documentation.
Search excerpts are explicitly distinguished from full-page review. Standards
catalogues establish scope, not access to normative requirements. No paywalled
standard or restricted dataset has been copied into this repository.
