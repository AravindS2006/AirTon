display/panel controls, a side projection/nozzle, a top circular feature and a

A drawing title/reference numeral is not evidence of patent filing, a fabricated
part, actual optical function or validated dual-eye operation.

The layout also leaves the relationship between front eye-facing features and
side nozzle unclear. Do not infer optical/air paths, eye-to-nozzle distance or
which controls implement safety. The drawings have no engineering dimensions,
tolerances, material specification, pneumatic schematic, optical prescription,
fasteners, battery placement or controlled revision block.

Native CAD, STEP/STL exports, assembly drawings and a dimensional/optical stack-up
remain unrecovered. The single-channel feasibility rig should precede a home-use
enclosure freeze. Use the recovered views as input to a design review, with
device positioning, cleaning, light shielding, accidental firing, low-vision
interaction, child access and serviceability considered explicitly.

The older July deck depicts a different bilateral concept. Neither visualization
defines the as-built instrument. Resolve the historical alternatives in a dated
mechanical/optical/pneumatic design decision with approved interfaces and tests.
