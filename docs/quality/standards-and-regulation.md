Review date 2026-10-04. Catalogue scope review only; full normative standards have
not been reviewed. Exact editions, national adoption, recognized status and
applicability must be assessed for the chosen intended use and market.

Do not report compliance from this matrix. For each applicable reference create
a clause-to-requirement/test crosswalk after obtaining the relevant text. Keep
draft notifications distinct from enacted rules. Record reviewer, interpretation,
required evidence and outstanding questions in the regulatory file.
