# Evidence-driven roadmap

Time estimates are planning horizons, not promises of clinical approval. The first
90 days are a suggested research window; actual work depends on team capacity,
procurement, collaborators and bench feasibility.

| Stage | Suggested work window | Deliverables | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0: Reconcile baseline | Weeks 1-2 | Native-file inventory; clinician-reviewed intended use; ownership and roles | Team signs off scope, claims and available artifacts |
| 1: Observe the signal | Weeks 2-6 | Shared fixtures; reflection/imaging/interference comparison; timing and cost budget | Reviewed observable and uncertainty/go-no-go result |
| 2: Instrumented bench prototype | Weeks 6-12, if feasible | Single channel, independent controls, raw acquisition and calibration record | Bench agreement/repeatability and fault evidence against approved criteria |
| 3: Clinical study preparation | After bench/safety gate | Protocol, statistics, consent/data plan, trained operators and required permissions | Authorized supervised study and frozen design/analysis |
| 4: Paired-data and usability studies | Approval-dependent | Measurements and all failures; subgroup and operator evidence | Independent review of agreement, safety and use limitations |
| 5: Risk-model research | Data/claim-dependent | Approved fundus/clinical pathway; external-domain evaluation; model card | Evidence supports the specific intended risk claim |
| 6: Bilateral / field / cloud integration | Measurement and workflow gates | Bilateral comparison, offline encrypted delivery, assigned clinician review | Physical, privacy, workflow and usability acceptance |
| 7: Release and surveillance | Regulatory/evidence-dependent | Required submissions, labeling, service, monitoring and change plan | Authorized release decision; no gate closed by simulation alone |

Do not schedule a commercial launch before the intended-use evidence and regulatory
path are resolved. Quarterly team reviews should examine unresolved measurement
and cost risks before expanding app or cloud scope.
