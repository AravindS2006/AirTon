About AirTon

AirTon is more than just a tool; it's a commitment to your vision health. We leverage the power of artificial intelligence to provide accessible and accurate eye disease detection. Our mission is to empower individuals with the knowledge and resources they need to proactively manage their eye health and prevent vision loss.

Our AI-powered platform analyzes fundus images, providing early detection of glaucoma and other conditions. We believe that early detection is key to effective treatment and preserving your sight.
