# Service, surveillance and complaint process

Proposed operating process for the home-monitoring product. No field deployment,
complaint history or incident reporting capability has been established.

A loan/rental or sale needs traceable device records, patient/clinic assignment,
inspection, calibration and cleaning controls. Define when a device is out of
service, how the patient is informed, replacement/return handling and data/access
transfer. Derive calibration interval and allowable drift from evidence rather
than selecting a convenient annual schedule.

Record complaints with received time, reporter channel, affected device/session/
software/model, description, potential harm, immediate containment and follow-up.
Restrict contact/health data. Triage technical fault, use error, inaccurate result,
late/missing delivery, inappropriate interpretation, cybersecurity and service
issues. Escalate clinical safety concerns to the designated qualified owner.

Assess reportability under the actual market rules; retain decisions and submitted
reports. Define correction/recall/field-action responsibilities, affected serial/
lot discovery, communication approval, effectiveness checks and regulator
interaction. A repository template cannot perform these obligations.

Trend failures, calibration drift, repeat attempts, subgroup/operator issues,
support burden, missed uploads and clinician workflow problems. Feed signals into
risk and change control. An ML update, hardware substitution or new home-use
population can require new evidence, labeling and regulatory assessment.

Before commercial launch, exercise one simulated complaint, one affected-lot
trace and one backup/restore/revocation scenario with fictional records. Record
results and gaps. No real patient record is required for this process exercise.
