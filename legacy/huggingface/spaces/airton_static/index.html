About Airton

Airton is a startup dedicated to revolutionizing eye health through innovative technology. We are committed to providing individuals with accessible and accurate tools for early detection and proactive management of glaucoma and other eye diseases. Our AI-powered medical device offers a convenient and reliable way to analyze fundus images, empowering users to take control of their vision health.

We believe that early detection is key to preventing vision loss. Airton's technology is designed to be user-friendly and insightful, bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI and everyday eye care.
