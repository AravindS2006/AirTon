About Us
Our venture aims to address the challenges faced by patients by
developing a portable, non-invasive device that utilizes air puff
technology to measure IOP. This solution will offer a more patient-
friendly approach, reducing discomfort while enabling frequent
monitoring outside of clinical environments. By making glaucoma
detection and IOP measurement more accessible, our device has the
potential to revolutionize early diagnosis and long-term management
of the disease, ultimately reducing the global burden of blindness
caused by glaucoma.

Glaucoma is a leading cause of irreversible
blindness worldwide, characterized by elevated
intraocular pressure (IOP) that damages the optic
nerve.
Current methods for measuring IOP are invasive,
uncomfortable, and predominantly limited to
clinical settings. 
This restricts access to timely and frequent
monitoring, particularly for people in remote or
underserved areas.
Context
Traditional IOP measurement techniques, such
as Goldmann applanation tonometry, require
direct contact with the eye. This process is
uncomfortable, can cause infection, and must
be performed by trained professionals,
restricting the frequency of testing. The lack of
portable, non-invasive solutions further
exacerbates accessibility challenges, hindering
early detection and monitoring of glaucoma.

Competition Analysis
Affordability: With an estimated price range of ₹ 20,000– ₹ 30,000, your device is
positioned as one of the most affordable options, making it accessible to a broader
market compared to competitors like iCare Home2 ( ₹ 2,00,000– ₹ 2,50,000) and
Triggerfish ( ₹ 3,50,000+).
Non-Invasiveness with Unique Technology: While many competitors rely on air puff
(Nidek, Reichert), your venture’s integration of interference patterns to calculate IOP is
an innovative approach that might offer higher accuracy or better patient comfort.
Portability & Accessibility: Similar to Tono-Pen Avia, your device offers portability, but at a significantly lower price, which
gives it an edge in accessibility for both clinical and home use.
Data Management: One of the key differentiators is your device’s ability to transfer data and create a patient database.
This feature can provide valuable insights over time and improve glaucoma management—a feature that is not as
advanced in other competing products & a feature many traditional tonometers lack.
Target Market: While competitors often focus on clinical environments or specialized cases, our venture addresses a larger
market, including people with high screen time and eye strain, which could lead to early detection and intervention for
glaucoma. This approach targets a growing demographic not directly addressed by the other competitors.

MVP
Front view Left Side view Back view Right Side view Top view
Our MVP is a dual-eye, portable, non-invasive device designed to
measure intraocular pressure (IOP) simultaneously in both eyes
using air puff technology. This innovative approach enhances
efficiency, accuracy, and patient comfort, addressing key
limitations of existing single-eye tonometers.
Key Features of the MVP:
Dual-Eye IOP Measurement: Simultaneous assessment
for improved accuracy and reduced testing time.
Portable & Non-Invasive Design: Compact and easy to
use, enabling monitoring outside clinical settings.
Air Puff Technology: Ensures a quick, painless, and
contact-free measurement.
Basic User Interface: Simple controls for ease of use by
medical professionals and potential home users.
Prototype Validation: Focused on testing measurement
accuracy, consistency, and reliability.
