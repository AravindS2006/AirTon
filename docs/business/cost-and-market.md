# Cost and market planning

All financial figures below are **historical deck assumptions or illustrations**,
not supplier quotes, forecasts or confirmed commercial outcomes. Review pricing,
taxes, service, financing and regulatory costs before a funding decision.

## Separate the unit and service businesses

For a hardware unit use:

`contribution = net realized hardware revenue - landed unit cost - variable sales/service cost`

`break-even units = ceiling(annual fixed costs / contribution)`

As an arithmetic check of page 34 only: revenue INR 35,000, unit cost INR 25,000
and annual fixed cost INR 1,259,000 give contribution INR 10,000, 28.57% contribution
margin and **126 units** after rounding up. This excludes additional variable costs,
tax treatment, finance, returns and uncertain development expenditure. It is not
evidence that AirTon can be made at INR 25,000 or break even after 126 sales.

Keep consulting/subscription revenue and costs separate; allocating them to a
hardware selling price obscures unit economics. Any future service estimate needs
paid clinician time, platform usage, support, consent/records administration and
retention/churn assumptions measured in consistent periods. Deck page 35 mixes
12.5% and 12.5 in its reciprocal lifespan calculation; rebuild rather than reuse it.

## Build a market estimate from reachable purchasers

Start with a defined intended workflow and geography, such as supervised clinics
in Chennai with a clear ophthalmology referral partner. Enumerate eligible clinic
types, operators, procurement budgets, test volume, device capacity, maintenance
capacity and reachable distribution. Record each input's source/date and uncertainty.

Distinguish potential patients, sites, devices per site and actual paying customers.
An estimate of disease prevalence cannot directly be called a device-sales market.
Reconcile the deck's 4,860 annual funnel customers with its 180-unit financial
scenario before reuse. Collect current authorized supplier quotes for comparator
devices; do not retain unattributed ratings or INR price ranges as current evidence.

## Decision record

Before approving a target price, record optical approach, quoted recurring BOM,
assembly yield, calibration labor, tooling amortization, service/recalibration,
warranty, training, distribution and cloud/care costs. Compare at least low/base/high
volume and yield assumptions. Preserve each scenario version and the input quotes.
No procurement, loan, external funding application or commercial launch has been
performed by repository creation.
